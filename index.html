System Reference

Instrument Field Manual & Glossary

1. Primary Transducer & Dial Indicators
Airflow Velocity Dial270° Scale
Displays instantaneous wind speed relative to the active unit's full scale. The dial automatically expands its upper limit if gusts exceed the default range.
Δ (Delta Velocity)Δv = v₂ − v₁
Shows the change from the previous reading. Green ▴ indicates rising wind speed; amber ▾ indicates subsiding airflow.
Beaufort Scale (0–12)12-Bar Meter
Standard meteorological scale classifying wind intensity from Force 0 (Calm, < 0.3 m/s) up to Force 12 (Hurricane, ≥ 32.7 m/s).
Status & System ClockLive / Sync
Indicates live sensor telemetry connectivity (Live, Acquiring, Standby, or Offline) alongside the synchronized local 24-hour clock.
2. Derived Aerodynamic Physics
Dynamic Pressure (q)q = ½ρv² (Pa)
Kinetic pressure exerted by moving air against a surface in Pascals, computed using standard atmospheric air density (ρ = 1.225 kg/m³).
Kinetic Flux (P/A)P/A = ½ρv³ (W/m²)
Wind power density—the rate of kinetic energy passing perpendicularly through 1 square meter of cross-sectional area per second.
3. Calibrated Velocity Scale Units
m/s (Meters per Second)Base SI Unit
Native scientific SI velocity unit received from the anemometer sensor and used for all underlying physics equations.
km/h (Kilometers per Hour)m/s × 3.6
Standard meteorological surface wind unit widely used in regional weather reports and ground transport operations.
mph (Miles per Hour)m/s × 2.23694
Imperial velocity unit commonly used in aviation surface reports and structural wind-load benchmarks.
knots (Nautical mph)m/s × 1.94384
Aerospace and maritime standard equal to 1 nautical mile (1.852 km) per hour.
ft/s (Feet per Second)m/s × 3.28084
Ballistics and fluid-dynamics engineering unit measuring linear airflow distance in feet per second.
4. Data Recorder & Statistical Metrics
Velocity vs. Time GraphHistory + Live
Automatically loads previously stored points from the database on open and plots incoming speeds against the horizontal Mean (μ) line.
Graph Options (⋯)Sampling Menu
Switch chart logging between Live, 15 Seconds, 2 Minutes, or 10 Minutes, clear the buffer, or export a full CSV dataset.
Mean Velocity (μ)μ = Σv / n
Arithmetic average of all wind speed readings recorded across n samples during the active monitoring session.
Min / Peak Gust / LastSession Extremes
Tracks the lowest speed, maximum recorded peak gust with timestamps, and the latest raw packet in m/s and Pascals.
